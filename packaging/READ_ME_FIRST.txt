DOCUMENT REDACTOR
=================

Removes confidential information from PDFs while keeping their layout.
Everything happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.


INSTALLING
----------
Drag "Document Redactor" into your Applications folder (or anywhere you like).


OPENING IT THE FIRST TIME
-------------------------
The app isn't registered with Apple, so the first time you open it macOS
will warn you that it can't check it. This only happens once.

  macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer:
    1. Double-click the app. When the warning appears, click "Done".
    2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
    3. Scroll down. Next to the message about "Document Redactor",
       click "Open Anyway", then confirm.

  macOS 14 (Sonoma) or older:
    1. Right-click (or Control-click) the app and choose "Open".
    2. Click "Open" in the dialog.

After that, just double-click it like any other app.


USING IT
--------
  1. Drop a PDF on the in-tray (or click it to pick a file).
  2. Mark what to hide: drag across words, or use the AREA tool for
     signatures, photos and logos. BLACKOUT covers with ink; SCRAMBLE swaps
     letters and numbers for random ones so the format stays visible.
     Use "Withdraw page" to remove whole pages.
  3. Click SANITIZE, check the inspection report, then
     "Download sanitized PDF" to save the cleaned copy.

Closing the window erases the document from memory.
